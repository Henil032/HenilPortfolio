GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

I'm Heritage Adigun, a web developer passionate about creating modern, responsive, and interactive websites.

I didn't have the opportunity or privilege to learn coding and web development as early as I would have liked. However, starting later didn't stop me from pursuing my interest in technology.

I've taken the time to learn, practice, experiment, and build projects that have helped me develop a good foundation in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Today, I'm confident in what I know and, more importantly, I'm aware that there's always more to learn. I enjoy turning ideas into functional websites, experimenting with different designs, and challenging myself to improve with every project I build.

My goal is to build on what I currently have, create meaningful and professional digital experiences, and eventually take my skills to a much higher level.

Outside of coding, I enjoy playing chess and I'm currently learning Spanish. I believe that progress isn't about how early you start—it's about how committed you are to keep learning and moving forward.
